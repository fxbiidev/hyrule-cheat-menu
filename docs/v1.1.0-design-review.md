# v1.1.0 design review

**Status: design approved on 15 November 2026.** The owner requested completion of v1.1 after the final uppercase subtitle change. This document preserves the earlier prototype evidence; current backend/release status is in [PROGRESS.md](../PROGRESS.md). This is a functional UI prototype on
`feature/v1.1.0-emerald-prototype`, based on published v1.0.0 commit `2553f76`.
The owner liked the initial design and requested the three-tab layout and new subtitle below. The three-tab layout and uppercase subtitle are approved.

## Proposed appearance and navigation

The renderer uses a 420 × 288 logical canvas with deep emerald panels, gold
accents, a distinct selection highlight, readable state indicators, a contextual
description card and controller hints. The identity remains **HYRULE CHEAT MENU**
and the exact credit remains `Created by fxbii.dev`. The subtitle now reads
`STANDALONE CHEAT MENU FOR OOTR`, replacing the former style label.
The owner requested uppercase after revision 2; the captures below retain the
preceding mixed-case subtitle and their original binary provenance.

The three main tabs are **CHEATS | FAST TRAVEL | ITEM MANAGER**. LB/RB cycles
backward/forward with wrap, and each tab remembers its selection and scroll.
Cheats has four collapsible categories: Player Cheats, Movement Cheats, Combat
Cheats, and Camera & World. Damage Multiplier remains directly below One Hit Kill.
Item Manager is a separate tab with Weapons, Shields, Equipment, Consumables and
Ammunition groups. Its former Inventory Tools category/submenu entry is removed.
B collapses the selected item category or closes from a collapsed root; R3 closes
and reopens the same tab. Fast Travel retains its region hierarchy, Favorites,
age selection, Return and confirmation flow. Selected travel age is displayed
in the context-card header so all three tabs fit comfortably.

The four new features are interface previews at this checkpoint. Their preview
controls do not apply combat damage, detach the camera or change inventory.
Their gameplay backends and save safety are not claimed as implemented or tested.
