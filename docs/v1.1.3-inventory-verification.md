## First seed acquisition

The fresh-game native test found that giving the Fairy Slingshot alone can leave `DekuSeed` capacity 0. The required related bag actor is `Item_BulletBag_Small`. Its native acquisition establishes base capacity 30 and its `IsItemStockMaxIfGet` behavior fills seed stock to 30. Treating only the changed capacity as completion would race that deferred stock update.

After separate confirmation, the corrected path publishes the existing Save/travel hold and gives one `Item_BulletBag_Small` through the native Give interface. It waits until actual native capacity and seed stock are both 30. A requested target of 30 then completes; a smaller positive target dispatches one native Consume for `30 - target`, and waits for actual requested stock before acknowledging success. The original two-second deadline covers both phases. A delayed or ambiguous phase is not replayed; existing timeout/uncertainty behavior requires a normal reload. The path rechecks age, save epoch, actor/world state and Slingshot ownership while pending.

The UI marks the prospective base capacity and explains native 30-seed bag initialization in the confirmation. Cancelling makes no bag or stock change. Acquired 40/50-seed bags stay on the ordinary quantity-edit path and are never replaced by the base bag. This is a related inventory prerequisite, without direct save writes or automatic Slingshot/equipment grants. Final-module emulator acceptance is recorded separately.

## Progression separation

The primary shop flow explicitly updates the bean purchase counter, `Get_MagicBeans`, and its shop follow-up state. The planting flow separately invokes the SmallFarm planting operation and removes one `Item_MagicBean` through the pouch system. Planted locations use independent persisted booleans referenced by `IsPlantedMagicBean_SmallFarm`.

These separate mechanisms support using the ordinary pouch Give/Consume path for stock editing without running a purchase or planting event. That safety conclusion is an inference from the native structure and event flows; before/after native progression measurements are needed to establish observed preservation. The Item Manager does not write those counters or flags, dispatch either event, or require a previous bean purchase.

## v1.1.3 policy

- Beans accept native capacity 10 only. Seeds accept native capacity 0/30/40/50. For Young Link with zero seeds, raw native capacity 0 and exactly one owned Slingshot, the snapshot marks `needs_seed_bag` and offers prospective base capacity 30. This does not write inventory. All other unexpected capacity states are refused.
- Young Link can confirm edits. Adult Link can inspect these child-use entries, with incompatible edits refused.
- Seeds require the separately owned Fairy Slingshot before increasing stock. A first grant with the missing base bag explicitly confirms its initialization through native `Item_BulletBag_Small` acquisition. The manager does not automatically grant the Slingshot or unrelated equipment. Existing 30/40/50 capacities are preserved; removing seeds empties ammunition while preserving the tool and bag.
- Beans can be added from zero without prior purchase, adjusted within 0–10, and removed to zero. Planting and consumption remain native gameplay operations.
- Infinite Items must be OFF during confirmed edits. Its existing eligible seed refill policy remains; beans stay excluded from both refill and ammunition-use protection.
- Separate confirmation, stale-snapshot checks, one dispatch per native phase, deferred acknowledgement, Save/travel holds and the reload-required uncertainty latch remain in force. Normal Save/Load persistence and actual item use require the separately recorded native tests.

The approved UI, navigation and existing catalog entries retain their established mechanisms. No game binaries, extracted asset exports, saves or private inspection utilities are distributed.
