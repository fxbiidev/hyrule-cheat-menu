## Supported recovery limitation

If the native engine refuses recovery while airborne or clipped, Fly remains visibly ON with the menu available. Descend under Fly to safe ground. Below a floor, keep Fly on, enable Noclip, ascend into clear space, restore Noclip collision, then turn Fly off. This is a verified escape path, not a guarantee of automatic anchor teleportation. Save holds remain until movement/recovery/protected landing has safely ended.
